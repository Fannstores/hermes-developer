# Requirements & Specification

Turn ambiguous requests into explicit, testable requirements before implementation.

## Rules
- Identify the goal, constraints, inputs, outputs, acceptance criteria, and non-goals.
- Preserve stated user intent; do not invent requirements.
- Resolve material ambiguity during planning, not after implementation.
- Prefer small, verifiable specifications over vague prose.
- Convert important requirements into tests or explicit verification checks.

## Definition
A requirement is complete when another developer can implement and verify it without guessing.
